# Public boundary

Public method, examples and reference checks are not an operational service, independent panel, proof of truth, adoption, empirical effectiveness or professional validity. Do not include private manuscript/canon, identifying profiles, personal mirror data, clinical records, credentials, unpublished sources or confidential deliberations. No actual person's invented speech is evidence, nor a diagnosis or factual assertion of their motives. Label fictional performance, avoid invasive identification, protect consent, and use functional roles or insufficiency when basis is absent. Real decisions and external actions require separate authorization and actual capabilities. No hidden persistence. Do not publish a link until the target exists and has been checked. Document limitations honestly.
